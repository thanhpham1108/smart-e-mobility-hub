**Scope:** UC-P01 through UC-P07, including UC-S02 and the no-show policy (FR-NI-13).

**Design:** [Class diagram](../class-diagram/parking/03-parking-private-ev.png) · [Editable source](../class-diagram/parking/03-parking-private-ev.puml) · [Test cases](../test-cases/03-parking-private-ev.md)

## Inputs and results

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Session` | Authenticated identity from Hub & Identity. Student actions derive the owner from the session. `ParkingSession` identifies the actual visit. |
| `ParkingDraft`, `ParkingReview` | The draft supplies Hub, vehicle and arrival/departure instants. The review returns these values, whole-window availability, exact check-in deadline and a reference to the reviewed policy and inputs. A review holds no capacity. |
| `ChargingDraft`, `ChargingRequest` | The draft identifies the parking reservation or session and contains current/target battery percentages and requested start/end. Smart Charging owns the returned request, its status and any assigned slot. |
| `BookingFilter`, `BookingSummary`, `BookingDetails` | An optional status/type restriction, a list row, and an owned record with current links and allowed actions. Details include the revision of the cancellation or checkout consequences when applicable. |
| `ReviewedAction` | Record reference, the revision of the summary the Student confirmed, and a key for retrying that action. The revision covers the affected requests, statuses and allocations. |
| `ArrivalProof` | Selected reservation reference or verifiable QR ticket plus the confirmed Hub and vehicle. A ticket lookup still requires an authorized owner. |
| `DateTime`, `key` | Compare instants using authoritative server time. Display Hub-local times (GMT+7). A key identifies one submission by the authenticated Student. |

## Operations

For Student operations, verify the current role and ownership before revealing records or returning a previous result. At each mutation, serialize the final eligibility check and all dependent writes in the shared application process. For a lost response, resolve the original key before retrying. Reusing a key for different input is a conflict. On a failed write, preserve the prior committed state.

| Class.method signature | Purpose | Input and output | Preconditions | Postconditions / state change | Errors / exception result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ParkingService.reviewReservation(actor: Session, draft: ParkingDraft): ParkingReview` | UC-P01 / UC-S02. Review one continuous interval. | Student session and draft → availability, summary and deadline. | Serviceable Hub, identified vehicle, permitted time points and arrival before departure. | Reconcile due holds and check each slot in [arrival, departure) against usable Hub capacity, outstanding holds and occupancy. A checked-in visit counts once. On a feasible interval, show the deadline capped at departure. | Identify invalid fields or an unavailable slot. With unverifiable capacity, report unavailable data. On request, query HubService for alternatives and review the selected Hub afresh. |
| `ParkingService.reserve(actor: Session, review: ParkingReview, key: String): ParkingReservation` | UC-P01. Confirm the reviewed interval. | Student session, confirmed review and submission key → booking with reference. | Review matches Student inputs and applicable policy. At commit, the deadline is still in the future and every covered slot has capacity. | Create one Confirmed reservation, snapshot the grace period and deadline, and hold one unit in every covered slot together. Show the exact deadline on the ticket. | Changed policy requires a new review and confirmation. A full slot rejects the whole booking. A replay returns the original booking. |
| `ParkingService.submitCharging(actor: Session, request: ChargingDraft, key: String): ChargingRequest` | UC-P02. Submit charging needs to Smart Charging. | Student session, charging draft and key → linked request. | Same owner, Hub and vehicle. Hub accepts charging. Parking is Confirmed before expiry or has an Active visit. Require 0 ≤ current < target ≤ 100 and a positive window within parking, restricted to the remaining permitted interval for an Active visit. | Recheck eligibility against concurrent cancellation, expiry and checkout. Smart Charging returns a Scheduled request with allocation, or saves a valid capacity-blocked request as Waiting without allocation and returns its Hub queue position. Insufficient charging time is rejected without saving. | Deny ineligible parking or invalid input. Charging times need not align to parking slots. Recover an uncertain submission through its key. |
| `ParkingService.listBookings(actor: Session, filter: BookingFilter): List<BookingSummary>` | UC-P03. List the Student's parking and charging history. | Student session and filter → matching summaries. | Active Student session. | Reconcile due records and read only the owner's reservations, visits and requests. Include Hub, vehicle, interval, status, check-in deadline where relevant and any charging allocation. An empty match is a successful result. | Distinguish a read failure from an empty list. Where expiry cannot complete, show pending cleanup or unavailable eligibility and suppress check-in. |
| `ParkingService.getBooking(actor: Session, ref: String): BookingDetails` | UC-P03. Open current details and prepare available actions. | Student session and parking or charging reference → details with linked records. | Record is accessible to the Student. | Reconcile due expiry. Show the latest parking and charging states, exact deadline and current actions. For cancellation or checkout, identify the dependent requests and allocations affected by confirmation. | Unknown or foreign references return the same unavailable result. Failed reads cannot authorize an action. |
| `ParkingService.cancelReservation(actor: Session, action: ReviewedAction): ParkingReservation` | UC-P04. Cancel unused parking and its eligible dependents. | Student session and confirmed summary → Cancelled reservation. | Owned Confirmed booking before expiry, no active parking or charging, and current reviewed consequences. | Smart Charging cancels linked Waiting/Scheduled requests and removes waiting entries from the queue. Commit their future allocation releases, the parking cancellation and all remaining slot releases together. Preserve history. | Return existing cancellation on replay. Refresh changed consequences for confirmation. Reconcile an overdue booking first. A blocked dependent prevents partial cancellation. |
| `ParkingService.cancelCharging(actor: Session, action: ReviewedAction): ChargingRequest` | UC-P05. Cancel only the selected charging request. | Student session and confirmed charging summary → cancelled request. | Owned Waiting or Scheduled request, with the allocation shown in the summary still current. | Smart Charging marks it Cancelled and releases its future allocation once. A Waiting request with no allocation is removed from the waitlist. Parking keeps its state and holds. | Return existing cancellation on replay. Refresh a changed assignment. Active charging needs the charging stop flow. Reject other terminal states. |
| `ParkingReservation.isCheckInWindowOpen(now: DateTime): boolean` | UC-P06 / FR-NI-13. Evaluate the stored time boundary. | Server instant → true exactly when arrivalTime ≤ now < expiresAt. | Reservation has a stored arrival time and expiry deadline. | Pure time comparison. Check-in separately enforces identity, status and allocation. | Missing or inconsistent timestamps fail closed and require reconciliation. |
| `ParkingService.checkIn(actor: Session, arrival: ArrivalProof, key: String): ParkingSession` | UC-P06. Admit the vehicle once against its booking. | Student session, arrival proof and key → Active visit. | Verify reference or QR, owner, Hub and vehicle. At the final decision, booking is Confirmed, its check-in window is open and the allocation can be honoured. | Assign one usable space, record unrounded actual arrival, create one Active session and mark the reservation Checked In. Charge the occupied space against the existing hold. Any gate simulation follows commit. | Reject early, mismatched or invalid admission. At or after the deadline, reconcile due expiry. Repeated admission returns the existing visit. Gate display failure only adds a warning. |
| `ParkingService.checkOut(actor: Session, action: ReviewedAction): ParkingSession` | UC-P07. Record departure and release the visit's capacity. | Student session and confirmed departure summary → Closed visit. | Owned Active visit for the selected vehicle, current consequences and no Active charging at commit. | Record unrounded actual departure, close the visit and mark parking Completed. Together release occupancy and all outstanding holds once, including unused time on early departure. Cancel unused Waiting/Scheduled requests through Smart Charging, including removal from the waitlist. Preserve charging history. | If charging is Active, offer a separate stop through Smart Charging: **Complete** ends the request as Completed; **Cancel** ends it as Cancelled. Once the stop is confirmed and the point released, refresh the summary and ask the Student to confirm checkout again. An unverified stop keeps parking Active and the point occupied. Declining checkout leaves parking Active. A replay returns the original completion. |
| `ParkingService.reconcileExpiry(reservationId: String): ParkingReservation` | FR-NI-13. Resolve a no-show on the timer or before an eligibility decision. | Internal reservation ID → current committed reservation. | Called by a scan at least once every 60 seconds, or while checking bookings, availability or a dependent operation. Use server time at the serialized decision. | At or after expiresAt, change an unused Confirmed booking to Expired, record NoShow and processing time, cancel linked Waiting/Scheduled requests, including waitlist entries and release outstanding holds/allocations together. Otherwise return its current state. A Checked In visit keeps its occupancy past departure until checkout. | An inconsistent Confirmed booking with active parking/charging, or failed cleanup, keeps its commitments. Flag for reconciliation, block overdue admission and new charging, and retry safely. Repeated scans release nothing twice. |

Capacity is booked by Hub and slot. Each reservation records its outstanding slot starts. Each private-EV charging request resolves to one reservation, directly or through its visit. Smart Charging also owns requests for fleet vehicles without a private parking reservation. The actual space is recorded on the visit at check-in, so different visits may use the same space over time, with at most one Active visit at a time. The default parking grid and default check-in grace are both 15 minutes and are independently configurable. On creation, store `expiresAt = min(arrivalTime + graceMinutes, departureTime)`.
